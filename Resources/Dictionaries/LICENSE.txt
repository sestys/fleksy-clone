Word lists
----------
cs.txt and en.txt are derived from
https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles 2018),
licensed under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Filtered to alphabetic entries only.

Name lists
----------
names-cs.txt and names-en.txt are generated by scripts/build-names.py from
public name statistics, which are records of fact rather than creative works:

  cs  Ministerstvo vnitra CR, frequency of given names and surnames in the
      Czech population, split by kind and gender. Taken from the UTF-8 copy at
      https://github.com/wilx/jmena, which preserves diacritics; the more widely
      cited https://github.com/lpavlicek/zxcvbn-czech copy is romanised and so
      is no use for a Czech keyboard.

  en  US Census 2010 surname file (public domain),
      https://www2.census.gov/topics/genealogy/2010surnames/names.zip
      Given names are not included: the subtitle corpus already covers them.

The frequencies in these files are not people counts. They are positions in a
band chosen so a name ranks sensibly among words; see scripts/build-names.py.
